Architecture

How SignBridge AI Works

A multi-stage pipeline designed for low latency, high stability, and zero unnecessary video bandwidth.

01
📹

Webcam Capture

Browser captures 30 FPS video locally via the WebRTC getUserMedia API. No raw video is streamed over the internet.

02
🖐️

MediaPipe Hands

MediaPipe tracks 21 skeletal landmarks per hand in 3D (X, Y, Z). Coordinates are normalized for scale and position invariance.

03
🧠

LSTM Neural Network

A rolling 30-frame sequence buffer feeds into a multi-layer Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) deep model running via WebSocket.

04
🗣️

Smart Sentence & Speech

Word confirmation logic prevents duplicates. Output is formatted with punctuation and converted into natural speech via TTS.

Core Capabilities

Engineered for Real-World Communication

⚡

Sub-200ms Latency

Asynchronous WebSocket streaming and lightweight LSTM architectures ensure instant visual feedback.

🛡️

Hold & Duplicate Protection

Prevents a held sign from repeating identically. A confirmation gate guarantees only intentional gestures become text.

📏

Scale & Translation Invariant

Landmark coordinates are normalized relative to wrist origin and palm span, accommodating different distances and angles.

🔊

Integrated Voice Synthesis

Natural text-to-speech with selectable voices, speech rate adjustments, and auto-speak on sentence completion.

🇮🇳

Indian Sign Language Vocabulary

Specifically designed and trained on ISL gesture grammar and two-handed vocabulary conventions.

📂

Dataset Studio & Extensibility

Includes complete scripts for recording custom gestures, training additional vocabulary, and model evaluation.
